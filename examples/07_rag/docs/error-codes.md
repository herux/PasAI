# PasDrone P1 Error Codes

E01: IMU calibration required. Place the drone on a flat, level surface and run IMU calibration from the app.

E04: Compass interference. Move away from metal structures, cars and reinforced concrete, then calibrate the compass.

E07: Motor overload. One or more motors are drawing too much current. Land immediately, power off, and check the propellers for damage or obstruction such as hair, grass or string. If E07 returns with clean propellers, contact service.

E12: Battery cell imbalance. See the battery guide.

E15: Weak GPS signal. Fewer than 8 satellites are locked. Wait in an open area until the status indicator turns green before taking off; return-to-home is unreliable until then.
