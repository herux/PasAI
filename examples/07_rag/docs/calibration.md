# PasDrone P1 Calibration

Compass calibration: open the app, choose Calibrate Compass, then rotate the drone horizontally 360 degrees until the indicator turns green. Next, hold the drone with its nose pointing down and rotate it 360 degrees again. Calibrate the compass when flying in a new location more than 50 km from the last one, or when the app shows E04.

IMU calibration: place the drone on a flat, level surface away from vibration. Choose Calibrate IMU in the app and follow the six positions shown on screen. Do not touch the table during each step. The full process takes about 5 minutes.

Gimbal calibration: if the horizon looks tilted in the video, choose Gimbal Auto Calibration while the drone rests on a level surface.
