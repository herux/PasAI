# PasDrone P1 Overview

The PasDrone P1 is a fictional compact camera drone used as sample data for the PasAI examples. It weighs 249 g with the standard battery, which keeps it under the registration threshold in many countries.

The camera records 4K video at 30 frames per second and takes 12 megapixel photos. A three-axis gimbal keeps the image steady in wind up to 10.7 m/s.

The remote controller connects over a 2.4 GHz link with a range of up to 6 km in open areas without interference. Range drops sharply near buildings, power lines and WiFi routers.

The P1 has three flight modes. Cine mode limits speed to 4 m/s for smooth footage. Normal mode allows up to 10 m/s with obstacle sensing active. Sport mode allows up to 16 m/s but disables obstacle sensing, so the pilot must keep a clear line of sight.
